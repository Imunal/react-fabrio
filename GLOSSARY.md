# React Fabrio domain

The language of personalizing printed and engraved products.

## Language

**Product**: A physical item offered for personalization, with a defined set of sides and decoration areas.

**Side**: A named view of a product, such as front or back, containing one or more decoration areas.

**Decoration area**: A physically measured region that accepts personalized content and produces a separate production artifact.
_Avoid_: Canvas, page, print zone.

**Template**: A reusable arrangement of elements with rules defining what a customer may change.

**Slot**: A labeled, editable position in a template for customer text or an image.

**Element**: A single positioned piece of text, an image, or vector artwork in a decoration area.
_Avoid_: Layer when referring to content rather than its stacking position.

**Design document**: The customer's editable personalization across all decoration areas of a product.

**Mockup**: A visual representation of personalized artwork on a product photograph or model.

**Production artifact**: A file containing artwork prepared for one decoration area's printing or engraving process.
_Avoid_: Preview, screenshot.

**Bleed**: Extra artwork outside the finished edge that accommodates variation during cutting or production.

**Safe margin**: An inset from the finished edge intended to protect important content from production variation.

**Effective DPI**: The source pixels available per physical inch of a placed image after cropping and scaling.
